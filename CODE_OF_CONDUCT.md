# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Toastify project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, gender identity and expression, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus on the technical issue rather than the person.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Respect the project's scope and shared resources.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or uncomfortable.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through their GitHub profile or another private contact method listed on the repository. Please include enough detail to help us understand what happened. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or take other appropriate action when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project channels.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
